⚙️ How CarIQ Works

Four steps from customer registration to analytical insight

Step 1
👤
Register a Customer
Enter the customer's financial profile — name, income, credit score, and employment type. CarIQ uses this to compute their monthly income and maximum affordable EMI.
Step 2
🚗
Log a Vehicle Enquiry
Match the customer to a vehicle they're interested in. This creates a lead in the pipeline — tracked from enquiry to conversion with dealer and date information.
Step 3
💳
Record a Transaction
When a sale closes, CarIQ computes the EMI automatically using the standard reducing-balance formula and stores it at transaction time for accurate historical analysis.
Step 4
📊
Analyse & Decide
The Python analytics pipeline extracts, cleans, and visualises all data across 5 business questions — affordability gaps, market penetration, velocity, and price sensitivity.

📖 What is What — Key Terms

Understand every field before you fill it in

💰 Annual Income
Your total yearly earnings in INR (Indian Rupees). Used to compute monthly income (÷12) and determine the maximum EMI you can safely afford. Example: ₹12,00,000 per year = ₹1,00,000/month.
📈 Credit Score (300–900)
A number representing your creditworthiness. 300 = poor, 900 = excellent. Most lenders require 650+ for loan approval. CarIQ analyses whether credit score actually predicts affordability (spoiler: income matters more).
📋 Lead
A record of a customer's interest in a specific vehicle. Status moves from New → In-Progress → Converted (sold) or Rejected. Time between enquiry and conversion is tracked as "days to close".
💳 EMI (Equated Monthly Instalment)
The fixed monthly amount paid to repay a car loan. Computed using: EMI = P × r × (1+r)ⁿ / ((1+r)ⁿ − 1). CarIQ flags EMIs that exceed 40% of monthly income as unaffordable (RBI guideline).
🚗 Vehicle Segment
CarIQ classifies vehicles into: Hatchback, Sedan, SUV, MUV, EV, Luxury, and Hypercar. Each segment has different average close times, price ranges, and affordability profiles.
📊 Affordability Rate
The percentage of loan customers whose EMI is ≤40% of their monthly income. CarIQ's current rate is 55% — meaning 45% of buyers are stretching beyond the safe lending boundary.
🌍 USD Normalization
Since CarIQ covers 7 countries with different currencies, all income and price comparisons are standardized to USD using FEDAI locked rates (₹93.5 = $1) set on Day 7 of the project.
⚡ Price Sensitivity
How much the eligible buyer pool changes when vehicle prices shift ±10%. CarIQ found near-zero sensitivity — income level, not price, is the binding constraint for most buyers.
