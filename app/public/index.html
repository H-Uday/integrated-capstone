📖 What is CarIQ?

A full-stack data analytics system for automotive sales intelligence

💳 EMI Calculator
Automatically computes monthly loan instalments using the standard reducing-balance formula used by Indian banks.
📊 Affordability Scoring
Flags customers whose EMI exceeds 40% of monthly income — the RBI standard safe lending threshold.
🌍 Global Coverage
Tracks transactions in India, USA, UK, UAE, Germany, Japan, Australia and Singapore with USD normalization.
🧠 Analytics Pipeline
A Python pipeline answers 5 business questions through interactive Plotly charts and Streamlit dashboards.
